# Getting an invite

Casama Cloud, the hosted service at casama.ai, is invite-only for now. That means a new account
can be made only for an email address that has been let in. Everyone who already has an account
keeps signing in as before.

## Join the waitlist

1. Get the app at **[casama.ai/download](https://casama.ai/download)** (or open
   **[app.casama.ai](https://app.casama.ai)** in your browser) and tap **Join the Waitlist** on the
   sign-in screen. Prefer not to install yet? Go to **casama.ai/waitlist**.
2. Enter your email address. You can add a line about what you would have a bot do first.
3. Joining the waitlist sends no email.

We let people in a few at a time, every day. When it's your turn you get one email, "You're in".
Then sign in with the same email address, using Google, Apple or the email link. A different
address won't work.

## Use an invite code

Someone may have sent you a code or a link.

1. In the app (or at [app.casama.ai](https://app.casama.ai)), tap **I Have an Invite** on the
   sign-in screen, or open your invite link / **casama.ai/waitlist**.
2. Enter the code and your email address.
3. Sign in with that email address, using Google, Apple or the email link.

You can also join the waitlist or use an invite code without installing the app:

- **Telegram:** send the code to [@casama_ai_bot](https://t.me/casama_ai_bot) as a message, then
  send your email. If you join the waitlist from Telegram, the bot messages you there when you're
  in. See [Chat apps](/guide/chat-apps#new-to-casama).
- **Email:** write to **get@casama.app** (put an invite code in the subject to skip the line). When
  you're let in, Casama emails you a fresh setup link. See
  [Email Casama](/guide/chat-apps#email-casama-get-casama-app).
- **Messenger:** message Casama's Facebook Page at
  [m.me/1539129842611601](https://m.me/1539129842611601) and send your email address. See
  [Casama in Messenger](/guide/chat-apps#casama-in-messenger).

In the app you can also enter the code first and then sign in with Apple or Google: the address
you sign in with is the one let in, even a hidden Apple address (Hide My Email). On casama.ai, with
Sign in with Apple, choose **Share My Email**: a hidden Apple address is a different address, so it
doesn't match the one you entered.

## Signing in with a different address

Your "You're in" email has a one-time invite code, like `K7M2-QXAB`. It lets in one other email
address, for example a hidden Apple address. Enter it with **I Have an Invite** in the app, or at
casama.ai/waitlist, then sign in with that address. It works once.

If a code has run out, you are put on the waitlist instead, near the front.

## Invite friends

Once you have finished your first chat, you can invite a few friends. Open **Settings → Invite
Friends** to find your code and your link. Each member gets a few invites (3 to start with). Your
friend enters your code with their email address, then signs in. You both get credits after their
first chat. See [Usage and credits](/guide/usage).

## If a sign-up is refused

You see: "Casama Cloud is invite-only for now. Join the waitlist or enter an invite code at
casama.ai/waitlist." No email is sent. Join the waitlist or enter a code, then try again with the
same address.
