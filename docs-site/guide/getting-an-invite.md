# Signing up

Casama Cloud, the hosted service at casama.ai, is open to everyone. There's no waitlist and you
don't need an invite. The Free plan costs nothing.

A [self-hosted](/self-hosting/) server's free personal license comes from signing in with a Casama
account too.

## Make an account

1. Get the app at **[casama.ai/download](https://casama.ai/download)**, or open
   **[app.casama.ai](https://app.casama.ai)** in your browser.
2. Continue with Google, Apple or your email address.

That's it: Casama makes your account, a first workspace and a first bot called Casama.

You can also start without installing anything:

- **Telegram:** send your email address to [@casama_ai_bot](https://t.me/casama_ai_bot). See
  [Chat apps](/guide/chat-apps#new-to-casama).
- **Email:** write to **get@casama.app**. Casama emails you a setup link. See
  [Email Casama](/guide/chat-apps#email-casama-get-casama-app).
- **Messenger:** message Casama's Facebook Page at
  [m.me/1539129842611601](https://m.me/1539129842611601) and send your email address. See
  [Casama in Messenger](/guide/chat-apps#casama-in-messenger).

To use the apps later, sign in with the same email address.

## Got an invite from a friend?

Open their invite link, or enter their code at [casama.ai/waitlist](https://casama.ai/waitlist)
together with your email address. Then sign up with that same address. You both
get credits after your first chat. See [Usage and credits](/guide/usage).

## Invite friends

Once you have finished your first chat, you can invite a few friends. Open **Settings → Invite
Friends** to find your code and your link. Each member gets a few invites (3 to start with). You
both get credits after their first chat.
