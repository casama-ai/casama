# Listing text for the release that adds the in-app browser

## What's new (iOS, Android, Mac — under 170 characters)

Your bot can now browse right inside Casama on your phone or Mac while you watch. Choose where to
open a site, approve risky steps, and take over anytime.

## A line for the description's feature list (phones, Mac, Linux)

- **A browser your bot can use inside Casama.** Watch bots work on a page right inside Casama on
  your phone, Mac or Linux computer, pick whether a site opens on your device or the bot's computer,
  approve risky steps, type your own passwords, and take over at any time.

## Not to claim

- Don't say it uses Safari, Chrome or Firefox sign-ins: it keeps its own separate cookie store inside Casama.
- On phones, don't say it works in the background: it works only while the chat is on screen.
- On Mac, it requires macOS 14 or later.
- On Linux, it uses the computer's WebKitGTK 4.1 (2.40 or newer); without it, Casama runs without the built-in browser.
- Don't name other companies' sites in screenshots.
