Casama gives you AI teammates that do the work, not just talk about it. Each bot has its own computer, remembers how you like things done, keeps going while you are away, and asks before it pays, sends or deletes anything.

Ask in plain words: book the trip, sort the inbox, research the market, build the site. The work comes back finished.

A computer of its own
Every bot works on a private computer with a browser, files and apps, kept apart from your own devices. Watch it work live, or take over the controls whenever you want.

You stay in charge
Sending, booking, paying and deleting wait for your OK. The bot saves a checkpoint first, so Deny undoes the work. Set a budget for each bot and see what every reply cost.

It works while you are away
Bots start on their own: on a schedule, when an email arrives, before a meeting, or when a web page changes. Wake up to a morning brief of what needs you.

It remembers
Facts, preferences and past work carry across every chat. Show a bot a task once and it keeps it as a skill. Everything it makes, from documents to sites and games, is kept in your Library.

A team, not one chatbot
Give each bot a role, tools and a budget. Put them in a group chat with family or coworkers, ask @everyone, and the lead bot splits the work. Start with more than 40 ready-made bots, from a personal assistant to a researcher, a coder and a travel planner.

Talk, text or call
Dictate a message, have replies read aloud, or call a bot live. Bots can also answer you in WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord and email.

Works with your apps
Connect Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Slack, Drive and many more. Logins from your password manager are filled in for the bot and never shown to the AI.

Open source, and yours
Casama is open source: the apps, SDKs, CLI and the safety rules that decide what a bot may do are public, so you can read exactly when a bot must ask you first. Bots run on Casama Cloud, where the AI models are picked for you and no keys are needed, or you can connect your own provider keys.

On your Wear OS watch
Casama has a Wear OS app. Ask a bot by voice, answer approvals and follow work in progress from your wrist. Add Casama tiles to your watch: Needs you (approve or deny requests), Ask (one tap to talk to a bot), Up next (your next routine, with Run now) and a tile for any one bot. Add Casama complications to your watch face: how many requests need you, your credits used, and a mic button that opens Ask.

The same chats on your phone, your computer, your watch and the web.

Casama Cloud has a Free plan. Paid plans are auto-renewing subscriptions that renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel them in Google Play, under Payments & subscriptions.
