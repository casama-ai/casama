Casama is an app for AI assistants ("bots"). Bots run on Casama's servers and their own cloud computers.

SIGN IN WITH THE DEMO ACCOUNT
1. Tap "Continue with Email", then "Use a Password Instead".
2. Enter the demo email and password, tap "Sign in". No code or two-step sign-in.
3. Agree that chats go to AI providers: "Agree and Continue".
To try registration (invite-only), use invite code REVIEW-XXXXXX.
The workspace is on the Free plan with Pro limits and $50 of credits; nothing needs buying.

WHAT TO TRY
- Chat: open the bot "Casama", ask "Compare three Italian restaurants in Lisbon in a table."
- Its computer: tap the monitor icon in the chat header > "Open Live View". "Take Control" asks for Face ID or passcode and pauses the bot.
- Approvals: sending, booking, paying or deleting asks first (Allow / Not Now).
- Calls: the phone icon in a chat starts a voice call.
- Routines: chat title > Bot profile > Routines ("Morning brief").
- Group chats: Chats > Group chats.
- Community: Settings > Help & support > Community (GitHub, Discord, ideas, status). After a week, one sheet asks for a GitHub star; no reward.

IN-APP BROWSER (new)
A WKWebView browser a bot uses on this iPhone while you watch.
1. In the "Casama" chat send: "Open example.com on my phone and tell me the heading."
2. A live card appears; tap it for the full browser with Stop and Take Over.
3. If a site needs sign-in or blocks the bot's computer, a card asks where to open it: "On this phone" or the bot's computer, with "Remember for this site".
- Works only while that chat is on screen; leaving stops it. Touching the page pauses the bot.
- Sending, posting, deleting or signing in asks first; purchases and password changes are left to the user. Password, card and code fields are never read.
- Banking, health, password manager and ID sites are off by default.
- Its cookies stay in a separate store on the device, never uploaded. Page text the bot reads goes to our server and the AI model, as disclosed on the AI providers screen.
- Settings > Device connections > Browser on This Phone limits bots or turns it off.

AI PROVIDERS (5.1.2(i))
Before anything is sent, a full-screen step names who gets what people share: OpenRouter and the model makers behind it (DeepSeek, Google, OpenAI), Google Gemini for calls, Parallel/Serper/DeepSeek for search. Withdraw in Settings > Privacy > AI providers.

APPLE HEALTH
Settings > Device connections > Health uses Apple's Health sheet, read-only. Daily summaries go to bots you allow; raw samples stay on the phone. Never used for ads.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
OpenRouter, Google Gemini Live, RevenueCat, Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, Composio, Firebase, PostHog (off in Settings > Privacy), ZeptoMail.

PURCHASES
Plus, Pro and Max (weekly, monthly or yearly) are auto-renewing subscriptions; credit packs are consumables, in Settings > Billing. Pro has a 1-week free trial for eligible new subscribers. The prices are intended (confirmed in our reply).

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
US storefront only: web checkout next to in-app purchase, and domain buying (Settings > Domains). Elsewhere: in-app purchase only. Not available in China mainland.

REMOTE SETTINGS
Server flags can turn off bot email, the welcome film, domain buying, the rating prompt, the star ask and the in-app browser. They only hide features.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings > Privacy > Danger Zone > Delete My Account. Please don't delete the demo account.

USER CONTENT
Long-press a reply > "Report reply". People, bots and shared pages can be reported; people can be blocked. Reports go to abuse@casama.ai

SIRI
Say "Ask Casama" or use Shortcuts (Ask a bot, Run a routine, What needs me). Signed in as the demo account, Siri reads the bot's reply.

PERMISSIONS
Microphone: dictation and calls, only when started. Calendar, contacts and location reach a bot only if turned on in Settings.
